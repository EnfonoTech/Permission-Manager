Approval Matrix — Setup & Working Guide

Account-driven approval routing on PM Workflow. Configure by tagging accounts, not by editing code — works for any company. This page explains how to set the fields and how every routing case behaves.

1. Overview 2. Key concepts 3. Tag an account 4. Purchase Invoice routing 5. Journal Entry routing 6. PO & Purchase Receipt 7. Approve / reject / rules 8. Change who approves 9. New company setup 10. Reference tables

1. Overview

Every purchase / expense transaction is routed to the right approver automatically. The route is decided by the account the money is booked to (for expense/service items and journals) or by the document type (purchase order, receipt, material invoice). Approvers are roles; the actual people are attached through Role Profiles, so changing an approver never touches the workflow.

Golden rule: you configure by data, not code. Tag your accounts with an Approval Group and assign people to Role Profiles. Nothing else needs editing.

2. Key concepts

TermWhat it is
Approval GroupA category on an Account (custom_approval_group): PayrollUtilitiesOfficeRentVehicle. It decides which department head approves an expense booked to that account.
Position roleThe approver, e.g. Vehicle Dept Head, Finance Manager, Accountant. Roles never change per person.
Role ProfileA bundle assigned to a user that grants a position role + base access. Change the person = reassign the profile.
Approval Group (on the invoice)A read-only header field auto-filled from the line account's group. The workflow reads this to route. You never set it by hand.

3. How to tag an account

This is the only setup step for expense routing. Open the expense/cost Account and set its Approval Group.

1
Go to Accounting › Chart of Accounts (or the Account list) and open an expense account, e.g. Fuel Expenses – Vehicles.
2
Set the Approval Group field (e.g. Vehicle) and save.
3
Optional: tag a parent account instead — all its child accounts inherit the group automatically. Tag a few parents, not every leaf.
Tagging by account is what makes this portable: a new company just tags its own accounts — no account numbers are hard-coded anywhere.

7. Approving, rejecting & the rules

1
Send for Approval — the creator sends the draft; it moves to Pending and waits for the routed approver.
2
Approve — only a user holding the routed role sees the Approve action. Two-level routes (service PI / JV) go to a department head first, then the Accountant.
3
Reject — returns the document for correction; a comment is required.
Built-in guards: a user cannot approve a document they created (no self-approval); a user without the routed role never sees Approve; attachments are enforced where required (Purchase Receipt, templated JVs).

8. Changing who approves

Approvers are roles, so you never edit the workflow to change a person:

1
Open the User and set their Role Profile to the position (e.g. Vehicle Dept Head).
2
To move the role to someone else, reassign that Role Profile. Approvals in flight instantly recognise the new holder.
A person can be assigned after a document is already sent for approval — the pending item appears in their inbox as soon as they get the role.

9. Setting this up for a new company

1
Install the app (bench migrate) — it creates the Approval Group field on Account and Purchase Invoice, and activates routing.
2
Tag the company's expense accounts with their Approval Group (or tag parent accounts). No numbers, no code.
3
Create the position Role Profiles and assign users.
4
The workflows are role-based — copy them across (fixture export/import) or rebuild; they need no per-company edits.
